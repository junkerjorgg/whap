# The Main Problems to Solve

1. We have a process, but we do not always follow that process, because it is too onerous, and those who are humble and loathe to slander in their own defense receive inadequate protection from those who lack these impediments and slander them first. The process is too onerous for those who recognize how rigorous it must be to preserve truth and justice, and who recognize how detailed their defense must be. When the actual process followed lacks that rigor and detail, it is a sham and not the true discipline of Christ’s church.

In short, lax, unaccountable courts reward slanderers and harm the scrupulously faithful.

To solve this problem, this app will help guide users through the process of defending against or pressing charges, enable them to prove and track whether the process is followed correctly, and help them gain the help of others rather than be left to their own resources alone.

2. The truth of one accusation can depend on the truthfulness of another. Such dependencies can be complex and so difficult to track and adjudicate. This app will facilitate tracking, relating, following, and summarizing the outcome of these dependencies.

3. It is difficult to find and evaluate precedents without relying on individual officers' memories alone. This app will provide a public record which can be examined to improve future judgments.
